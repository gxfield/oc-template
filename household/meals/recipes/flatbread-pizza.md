## Ingredients

### Base

- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 2 eggs
- Italian seasoning

### Toppings

- 1 cup salsa
- 1 cup cheese
- Cut up vegetables and protein of choice

## Instructions

1. Put cottage cheese, eggs, and Italian seasoning (not cheese) in a blender and blend for 1 minute
2. Pour liquid onto a lined (parchment paper) cookie sheet
3. Preheat the oven to 360°F and cook for 25-30 minutes
4. Let cool for 10 minutes
5. Add salsa, cheese, veggies, and protein
6. Bake for another 8-10 minutes or broil in the oven for 2-3 minutes

## Nutrition

- Calories: 296 kcal

- Carbs: 12g
- Protein: 24.5g
