## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2-3 cloves garlic
- 1 cup red or green peppers
- 8-10 lettuce shells
- Optional: cashews on top

**Sauce:**
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 Tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tsp sriracha
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp ginger

## Instructions

1. Brown ground turkey in a frying pan
2. Add onion, garlic, and peppers, fry for 5 minutes
3. Whisk sauce ingredients in a separate bowl
4. Add sauce to the meat mixture and evenly coat
5. Spoon meat into individual lettuce wraps and serve

## Nutrition

- Calories: 236 kcal (per serving, without cashews)
- Fat: 11.5g
- Carbs: 5.5g (net)
- Protein: 27.15g
