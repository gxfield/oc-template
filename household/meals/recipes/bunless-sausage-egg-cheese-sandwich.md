## Ingredients

- 3 fried eggs
- 2 slices of cheese
- 1-2 bacon or ham
- 1/2 avocado, sliced
- Optional: guacamole

## Instructions

1. Place sausages in a frying pan until fully cooked
2. Fry eggs in a frying pan and season with salt and pepper
3. Layer egg, bacon or ham, cheese, and avocado like a sandwich
4. Serve and enjoy
