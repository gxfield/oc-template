## Ingredients

- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 3-4 cups cut up vegetables (onion, bell pepper, zucchini, garlic)
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup almond flour
- 1/2 cup grated cheddar cheese
- Seasoning, salt, and pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients together
2. Put into an oven-safe casserole dish
3. Bake in oven at 380°F for 25-30 minutes

- Carbs: 7g (net)
