## Ingredients

- 2-3 eggs, scrambled
- 1/2 cup chopped veggies (onions, mushrooms, bell peppers)
- 2 slices of cheese
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1-2 coconut wraps or cottage cheese flatbread

## Instructions

1. Crack 2-3 eggs in a bowl and whip with a fork
2. Pour into a frying pan sprayed with avocado oil
3. Add veggies, salsa, and protein (optionally saute veggies first)
4. Cook for 3-5 minutes
5. Add mixture to flatbread or keto wrap, roll and enjoy

> **Note:** Add wraps or flatbread separately in Cronometer.
