## Ingredients

- 12 eggs
- 3 Tbsp cream (half and half, cream, sour cream, or milk)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup grated cheese
- 3-5 cups chopped veggies (onion, peppers, mushrooms, zucchini, garlic, sausage, and bacon)
- Optional garnish: basil, parsley, dill, salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Crack eggs in a mixing bowl, add dairy and salt
3. Whisk ingredients together, put half the grated cheese in with the egg mixture
4. Fry veggies and protein in a frying pan with 1 Tbsp butter for a few minutes until tender but not mushy
5. Let cooked veggies cool for a few minutes
6. Grease a 9x13 casserole dish
7. Stir egg mixture in with veggies and pour into casserole dish
8. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
9. Bake for 20-25 minutes
