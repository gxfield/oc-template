## Ingredients

- 3.5 cups shredded cooked chicken
- 5 cups chopped veggies (carrots, onion, celery, green beans, turnip, mushrooms, peas)
- 1 Tbsp garlic
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp grass-fed butter
- 1.5 cups organic chicken broth
- 1/3 cup cream
- 2 Tbsp almond flour
- Italian seasoning
- Thyme
- Salt and pepper
- Red pepper chili flakes

## Instructions

1. Put all veggies in a pan with 2 Tbsp butter and cook for 5-7 minutes
2. Add 1.5 cups organic chicken broth, 3.5 cups shredded chicken, and 1/3 cup cream
3. Add 2 Tbsp almond flour for thickening
4. Add seasonings (Italian, thyme, salt/pepper, chili flakes)
5. Simmer until thickened and heated through
