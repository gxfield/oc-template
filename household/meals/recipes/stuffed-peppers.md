## Ingredients

- 4-6 bell peppers, hollowed out
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1-2 cups mushrooms
- 4 Tbsp feta cheese
- Rosemary
- Thyme
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Wash and hollow out 4-6 bell peppers
2. Brown meat in a frying pan, add onions and mushrooms, cook until soft
3. Put 1 Tbsp feta cheese in each pepper and layer with meat mixture
4. Add 1 Tbsp feta cheese on top of each pepper
5. Cook in the oven at 400°F for 10 minutes (cook longer if you prefer softer peppers)
