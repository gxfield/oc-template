## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup organic raspberries
- 1 scoop protein (whey isolate)
- 2 Tbsp greek yogurt
- Fresh squeezed lemon
- Optional: 1 Tbsp MCT oil, chia seeds, psyllium husk

## Instructions

1. Add raspberries, protein powder, greek yogurt, and optional MCT oil and psyllium husk to a blender
2. Blend for 1-2 minutes until smooth
3. Top with 1 Tbsp of mixed berries

## Nutrition

_Excluding optional items._

- Calories: 202 kcal
- Fat: 1.5g
- Carbs: 16.3g
- Protein: 30g
