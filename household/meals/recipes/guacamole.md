## Ingredients

- 2 ripe avocados
- 1-2 garlic cloves
- Juice from 1-2 limes
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Cut avocados in half and remove pits
2. Mash avocado with a fork to desired consistency
3. Mince garlic and add to avocado
4. Squeeze in lime juice, season with salt and pepper
5. Mix well and serve immediately

## Nutrition

- Calories: 28 kcal
- Fat: 2.3g
- Carbs: 2.2g (net carbs 1.1g)
- Protein: 0.4g
