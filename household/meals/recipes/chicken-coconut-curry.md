## Ingredients

- 4 cooked chicken breasts
- 1 onion
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 Tbsp fresh grated ginger
- 1/2 cup broccoli or cauliflower
- 1/2 cup carrots
- 1/2 cup bell peppers
- 1/2 cup mushrooms
- 1 Tbsp curry powder
- 1 pinch cayenne
- 1 (14 oz) can coconut cream
- 1 cup organic vegetable broth
- Salt and pepper
- Coconut oil for cooking

## Instructions

1. In a saucepan, add coconut oil, onion, garlic, ginger, carrot, broccoli, peppers, and mushrooms
2. Cook for 3-4 minutes
3. Add cooked chicken, curry powder, cayenne, veggie stock, coconut milk, salt, and pepper
4. Reduce heat and cook for 10 minutes
5. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed
6. Serve with fresh lemon and lime (optional)
