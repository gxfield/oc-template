## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper
- Optional: Italian blend seasoning

## Instructions

1. Whisk together olive oil, dijon mustard, apple cider vinegar, and lemon juice
2. Season with salt, pepper, and optional Italian blend seasoning
3. Serve immediately or store in the fridge
