## Ingredients

- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1 bag frozen cauliflower rice
- 1/4 cup grated cheddar cheese
- 1 tsp each: salt, onion powder, garlic powder, pepper, oregano

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Cook cauliflower rice in a frying pan until tender
3. Add seasoning and make sure it is well seasoned
4. Blend 1 cup cottage cheese until smooth and add to cauliflower rice
5. On a lined (parchment paper) cookie sheet, form mixture into tater tot shapes
6. Bake for 35-40 minutes

> Tip: Add a small amount of sriracha sauce for dipping.
