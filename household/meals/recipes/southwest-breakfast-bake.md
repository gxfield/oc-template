## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey or beef
- 1 green bell pepper
- 1 red bell pepper
- 1 cup onion, chopped
- 1 Tbsp avocado oil
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper

### Sauce

- 1 (14 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 (4 oz) can green chiles
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp garlic, minced
- 1 tsp chipotle powder
- 6 eggs

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line a 9x11 casserole dish with parchment paper
2. Brown meat, add garlic, onions, and chili powder
3. Add bell peppers and saute until they begin to soften, about 2-3 minutes
4. Add mixture to bottom of casserole dish
5. In a heated frying pan, combine tomato sauce, diced green chiles, chili powder, chipotle powder, and cumin
6. Allow to come to a simmer, then remove from heat and add to casserole dish over the meat mixture
7. Crack 6 eggs evenly spaced over the top
8. Bake for 25 minutes
