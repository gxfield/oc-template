## Ingredients

- 1.5 cups cooked chicken breast
- 3-4 cups chopped veggies
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 to 1 onion
- 3-4 cloves garlic
- 3-4 Tbsp ginger
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce (coconut aminos)
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 cup water
- Optional: sriracha sauce

## Instructions

1. Chop veggies and add to a wok with 1 Tbsp olive oil along with cooked chicken
2. In a separate bowl, mix garlic, ginger, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sriracha
3. Pour liquid mixture over chicken and veggies
4. Steam and cook until veggies reach desired tenderness
