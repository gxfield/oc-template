## Ingredients

- 2 cups cottage cheese (blend in blender before using)
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- 2 tsp dried dill
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp dried onion flakes
- 1/4 tsp sea salt

## Instructions

1. Blend cottage cheese in a blender until smooth
2. Add dried parsley, dill, garlic powder, onion powder, dried onion flakes, and sea salt
3. Mix well and refrigerate before serving

## Nutrition

- Calories: 27 kcal
- Fat: 1.2g
- Carbs: 1g
- Protein: 3g
