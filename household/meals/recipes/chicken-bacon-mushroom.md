## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs chicken thighs or breasts (approx 4-5 chicken breasts)
- 2 slices bacon, chopped
- 2 cups mushrooms
- Salt and pepper
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1/3 cup organic chicken broth
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 Tbsp butter

**Thickening sauce:**
- 1/3 cup blended cottage cheese
- 1/4 cup cream
- 1 Tbsp almond flour (if needed)

## Instructions

1. Sear chicken in a frying pan with butter, salt, and pepper, browning 5-7 minutes each side
2. Put chicken in an oven-safe casserole dish
3. Add 1 Tbsp butter, bacon, garlic, and mushrooms to the frying pan
4. Cook until mushrooms are soft
5. Add chicken broth and thyme
6. Turn heat up to medium-high and add thickening sauce to the liquid
7. Pour sauce over chicken
8. Cook in the oven at 375°F for 10-15 minutes (depending on thickness)
9. Check that internal temperature reaches 165°F
