## Ingredients

- 1-2 cups chopped lettuce
- 1 colored pepper
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- 2 Tbsp shredded cheese
- 2 Tbsp guacamole
- 1/4 cup salsa
- 1 green onion
- 1 cup lean ground beef, ground turkey, or chicken (with taco seasoning)

### Taco Seasoning

- 1 Tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp celtic or rock salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- Dash of red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1. Brown meat in a pan and add taco seasoning
2. Chop all veggies and place in a bowl
3. Add cooked seasoned meat
4. Top with cheese, guacamole, and salsa
5. Add dressing of choice, toss together, and enjoy

## Nutrition
