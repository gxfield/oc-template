## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp chia seeds
- 1/2 cup almond milk or milk of choice
- 1/2 cup berry topping (blueberries, raspberries, blackberries)

## Instructions

1. Pour chia seeds and milk into a jar and mix well
2. Let rest for 3-5 minutes and stir again
3. Cover and store in the fridge for at least 2 hours or overnight
4. Top with your favorite fruit before eating

## Nutrition

_With almond milk._

- Calories: 220 kcal
- Fat: 12g
- Carbs: 9g (net)
- Protein: 7g
