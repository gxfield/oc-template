## Ingredients

- 1 steak
- 1 head of cauliflower
- Vegetables of choice
- Olive oil

**Jerk Sauce:**
- 1/2 scotch bonnet chile or fresno chile
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 shallot
- 1 scallion
- 1 inch of ginger
- 3/4 cup coconut aminos
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/2 tsp black peppercorns
- 1/2 tsp allspice
- 1 lime (zest and juice)

## Instructions

1. Combine jerk sauce ingredients
2. Use sauce to paint on the steak while cooking
3. Cover raw cauliflower with olive oil and bake at 350°F for 44-50 minutes
4. Remove cauliflower and paint on the jerk sauce
5. Bake for another 10-15 minutes
