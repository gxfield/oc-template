## Ingredients

- 1 avocado, chopped
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- 1/3 cup green onion, chopped
- 2 Tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
- Juice from 1 lime
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Chop avocado, cucumber, and green onion
2. Combine in a bowl
3. Drizzle with oil and lime juice
4. Season with salt and pepper, mix and enjoy

## Nutrition

Per serving:

- Calories: 157 kcal
- Fat: 14.25g
- Carbs: 4.25g (net)
- Protein: 1.75g
