## Ingredients

- 2 cups organic green beans
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 Tbsp grass-fed butter
- Salt and pepper
- 2-3 cloves garlic

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 390°F
2. Place veggies on a cookie sheet
3. Add melted butter, salt, pepper, and garlic
4. Cook in oven for 20-25 minutes

## Nutrition

Per serving:

- Calories: 70 kcal
- Fat: 3.7g
- Carbs: 6.7g (net carbs 4g)
- Protein: 2.3g
