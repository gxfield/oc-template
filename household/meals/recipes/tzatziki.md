## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup grated cucumber
- 1 cup greek yogurt (low sugar)
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 garlic clove
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp dill
- Optional: 1 tbsp mint

## Instructions

1. Grate cucumber and squeeze out excess moisture
2. Combine greek yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, minced garlic, salt, and dill
3. Mix in grated cucumber
4. Optionally add mint
5. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving
