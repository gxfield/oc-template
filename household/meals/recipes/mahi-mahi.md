## Ingredients

- 4 mahi mahi steaks
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Season mahi mahi steaks with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper
2. Put 1 Tbsp butter in frying pan over medium heat
3. Cook 3-5 minutes on each side
4. Spoon garlic butter on top and serve
