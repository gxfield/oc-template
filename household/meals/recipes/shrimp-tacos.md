## Ingredients

- 1 lb raw shrimp, tails removed
- 1 Tbsp butter
- Keto wraps or flatbread
- 2 cups coleslaw
- 1 lime for serving
- 1/4 cup green onions

**Shrimp Seasoning:**
- 1 Tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp celtic or rock salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- Dash of red pepper flakes

**Spicy Sauce:**
- 1/2 cup greek yogurt
- 1-2 Tbsp sriracha
- 1 lime, squeezed
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Pat shrimp dry with a paper towel
2. Toss shrimp in a bowl and mix with seasoning
3. Saute shrimp in a pan with a drizzle of olive oil for 5-8 minutes, flipping occasionally until cooked through
4. Mix spicy sauce ingredients together
5. Assemble tacos with shrimp, coleslaw, and sauce
