## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp spices (steak spice, garlic powder, salt, pepper)
- 1/4 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 onion, blended
- Flatbread recipe for buns (add separately)
- Toppings of choice (add to Cronometer)

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl
2. Form medium-sized hamburger patties
3. Cook thoroughly on the BBQ
4. Serve on flatbread buns with desired toppings

## Nutrition

- Calories: 121 kcal (per patty)

- Carbs: 1.9g (net)
- Protein: 12.5g
