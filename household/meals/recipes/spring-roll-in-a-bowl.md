## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground pork, beef, or turkey
- 1 bag coleslaw
- 1 cup bean sprouts (optional)
- 2-3 cloves garlic
- 2-3 Tbsp ginger root, grated
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce (Braggs)
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
- Pepper
- Lime for topping (optional)

### Sauce

- 3 Tbsp soy sauce mixed with 1/4 cup water
- 2-3 Tbsp grated ginger
- 2-3 cloves crushed garlic
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
- Generous pinch of pepper

## Instructions

1. Brown ground meat in a frying pan
2. Add a bag of coleslaw and bean sprouts
3. Mix sauce ingredients together
4. Pour liquid mixture over everything
5. Stir and coat sauce over coleslaw and sprouts
6. Steam for 5-10 minutes on low heat
