## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 cup each chopped veggies (carrots, celery, mushrooms)
- 1 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 Tbsp parsley
- 1 Tbsp pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 pkg coleslaw
- 4 cups beef broth
- 3 (8 oz) cans crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup water (if needed)

## Instructions

1. Brown meat and onions
2. Combine all ingredients in a large pot or instant pot
3. Simmer covered for at least 2 hours (or all day)
4. If using an instant pot, use a soup setting - ready in 30 minutes
