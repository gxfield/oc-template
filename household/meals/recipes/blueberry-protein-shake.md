## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup organic frozen blueberries
- 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
- 2 Tbsp greek yogurt
- Optional: MCT oil, psyllium husk, chia seeds

## Instructions

1. Add blueberries, protein powder, greek yogurt, and optional MCT oil and psyllium husk to a blender
2. Blend for 1-2 minutes until smooth
3. Top with 1 Tbsp of mixed berries

## Nutrition

_Excluding optional items._

- Calories: 190 kcal
- Fat: 3.5g
- Carbs: 15g
- Protein: 27.5g
