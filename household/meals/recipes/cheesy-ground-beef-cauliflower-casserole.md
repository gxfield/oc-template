## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 3 cups cauliflower rice
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 bell pepper
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp ground chipotle
- 1 (16 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup sliced pickled jalapenos

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a frying pan on medium heat
2. Add onion and bell pepper, cook about 3-5 minutes
3. Add beef and cauliflower, cook until beef is no longer pink (about 5-7 minutes)
4. Stir in garlic, chili powder, cumin, oregano, salt, and chipotle
5. Add tomatoes and their juices, bring to a simmer, cook for 3 minutes
6. Transfer to an oven-safe dish
7. Sprinkle cheese over the beef mixture and top with sliced jalapenos
8. Broil in the oven until cheese is melted and bubbly
