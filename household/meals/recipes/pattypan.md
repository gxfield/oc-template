## Ingredients

- 1 pattypan squash
- 3-4 mushrooms, chopped
- 4 slices of tomato
- 1/2 small onion, chopped
- 2-4 pieces of asparagus
- 1-2 slices of ham
- Olive oil for frying
- Salt and pepper (celtic or himalayan)
- Parmesan cheese, grated

## Instructions

1. Slice the pattypan into pizza-dough rounds, 1/4 to 1/2 inch thick
2. Rub both sides with olive oil and season with salt and pepper
3. Bake at 450°F for about 10-12 minutes on each side
4. Poke with a fork to test tenderness
5. When cooked, place sliced tomatoes on the base and add toppings
6. Saute asparagus, onion, and mushrooms with ham for toppings
7. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese
8. Place back in the oven for 7 minutes
