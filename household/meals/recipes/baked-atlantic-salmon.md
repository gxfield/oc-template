## Ingredients

- 4 salmon filets
- Olive oil for drizzling
- Pepper

### Sauce

- 1 cup greek yogurt
- 2 chopped up pickles
- 1/2 onion
- 1 bell pepper
- 2-3 Tbsp dill juice
- 1-2 Tbsp sriracha
- Pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F (or grill on BBQ - 6-8 minutes one side, flip for 2-4 minutes)
2. Mix sauce ingredients together
3. Place filets on a baking sheet, add a drizzle of olive oil and one heaping Tbsp of sauce
4. Add pepper and cook for 20-25 minutes until salmon is opaque and flaky
5. Smaller filets may only need 10-15 minutes
6. Garnish with parsley or dill
7. Serve with roasted vegetables and cauliflower rice
