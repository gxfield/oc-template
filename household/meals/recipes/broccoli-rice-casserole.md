## Ingredients

- 2-3 cups cauliflower rice
- 3 cups chopped organic broccoli florets
- 2 cups grated cheddar cheese (divided)
- 1 cup cream cheese
- 1 cup blended cottage cheese
- 1/2 cup half and half cream
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp cracked pepper
- 1/4 tsp mustard powder

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Add cauliflower rice and chopped broccoli to a large casserole dish
3. In a bowl, combine 2.5 cups cheddar, cream cheese, cottage cheese, cream, water, and spices
4. Stir well to combine
5. Pour mixture into a 9x9 baking dish
6. Top with remaining cheddar cheese
7. Bake uncovered for 30 minutes
