## Ingredients

- 3 cups cooked shredded chicken
- 1/4 cup mayo (Chosen Foods with avocado oil)
- 2 Tbsp dijon mustard
- 2 Tbsp fresh dill
- 2 Tbsp parsley
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup green onions
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Stir together mayonnaise, mustard, dill, parsley, and garlic until smooth
2. Add chicken and green onions
3. Season with salt and pepper to taste
4. If time allows, refrigerate for 2 hours to allow flavors to develop
