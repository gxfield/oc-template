## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup ice cubes
- 1 scoop chocolate protein
- 2 Tbsp greek yogurt (coconut)
- 1 Tbsp no-sugar-added peanut butter
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- 1 Tbsp raw cacao powder (unsweetened)
- 1 tsp chia seeds

## Instructions

1. Add ice cubes, cacao powder, peanut butter, vanilla, protein powder, greek yogurt, chia seeds to a blender
2. Blend for 1-2 minutes until smooth
