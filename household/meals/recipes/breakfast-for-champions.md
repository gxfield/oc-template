## Ingredients

- 3 eggs, scrambled
- 2 slices bacon
- 1 Tbsp cheese
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1 cup peppers and onion

## Instructions

1. Crack 3-4 eggs in a bowl and whip with a fork
2. Pour into a frying pan sprayed with avocado oil
3. Cook for 3-5 minutes
4. Add chopped up bacon, cheese, and salsa
5. Serve and enjoy
