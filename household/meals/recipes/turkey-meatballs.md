## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup almond flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp basil
- 1 tsp dill
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl
2. Form into 2-inch balls
3. Place in frying pan and brown for 8-10 minutes, turning often
4. Transfer to an oven-safe dish and bake at 375°F for 15-20 minutes

**Tips:** Serve with roasted veggies and cauliflower rice, and add tzatziki sauce
