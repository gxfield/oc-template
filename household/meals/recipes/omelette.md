## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 pieces bacon or sausage
- 1 cup chopped veggies (onion, peppers, mushrooms)
- 2 Tbsp feta cheese
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1/2 avocado, sliced

## Instructions

1. Crack eggs into a bowl and whip with a fork until well combined
2. Melt butter in a frying pan on low-medium heat
3. Pour eggs into the pan and cook without stirring
4. Use a spatula to push the edge of eggs slightly to the center to allow uncooked egg to move to the edge
5. Add 1 tbsp of water and cover for 30-45 seconds
6. Place all ingredients on half of the omelette
7. Fold in half and let cook for 30-45 seconds
