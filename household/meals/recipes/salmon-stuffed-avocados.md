## Ingredients

- 2 cans flaked salmon or 2 cups cooked shredded chicken (or egg)
- 2 avocados
- 1/2 cup plain greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup diced celery
- 2 Tbsp chopped parsley
- 1 Tbsp lime juice
- 2 tsp mayonnaise (Chosen Foods)
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cracked pepper
- Chopped chives and hot pepper flakes
- Optional: hot sauce

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine yogurt, celery, parsley, lime juice, mayonnaise, mustard, salt, and pepper; mix well
2. Add the salmon or chicken and stir until thoroughly combined
3. Halve avocados lengthwise and remove pits
4. Scoop about 1 tablespoon of flesh from each avocado half into a small bowl
5. Mash the scooped avocado with a fork and stir into the salmon or chicken mixture
6. Fill each avocado half with mixture and garnish with chives
