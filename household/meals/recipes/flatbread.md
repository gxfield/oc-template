## Ingredients

- 1 cup organic cauliflower
- 1 cup red onion (medium onion)
- 5-6 cloves of garlic
- 5 eggs
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 3-4 Tbsp lemon pepper seasoning
- 1 cup almond flour
- 1 Tbsp pepper
- 1.5 cups egg whites

## Instructions

1. Put all ingredients in a blender and mix well
2. Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper
3. Pour mixture onto cookie sheet
4. Place in the oven at 375°F for 30-35 minutes
