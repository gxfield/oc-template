## Ingredients

- 1 large head cauliflower or 1 bag frozen cauliflower rice
- Juice from 1 lemon
- 1/2 cup tahini
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 3 Tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup filtered water (as needed for texture)

## Instructions

1. Cook cauliflower until soft, then drain well
2. Add cauliflower, lemon juice, tahini, garlic, olive oil, cumin, and salt to a blender
3. Blend until smooth and creamy
4. Add filtered water as needed to achieve desired consistency
5. Taste and adjust seasoning

## Nutrition
