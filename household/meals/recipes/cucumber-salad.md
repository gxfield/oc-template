## Ingredients

- 6 cups cucumbers, sliced in circles
- 1/2 cup sour cream (lactose free if desired)
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp fresh dill
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 cup green onions
- 1 small red onion, chopped

## Instructions

1. Stir together sour cream, dill, olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic until smooth
2. Add cucumbers and red onions
3. Season with salt and pepper to taste

## Nutrition

- Calories: 118 kcal
