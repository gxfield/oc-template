## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/3 cup beef broth
- 28 oz crushed tomatoes
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1/4 tsp oregano
- 1/4 tsp ground thyme
- 1.5 tsp salt (or to taste)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/4 tsp ground pepper
- 1/4 tsp dried parsley
- 2 Tbsp basil, chopped (or 1 Tbsp dried)

## Instructions

1. Fry and brown the meat
2. Add onion and bell pepper, cook for 3-5 minutes until tender
3. Add garlic and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant
4. Stir in beef broth and allow to reduce for about 1.5 minutes
5. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, thyme, salt, bay leaf, and pepper
6. Remove bay leaf, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes
7. Serve over spaghetti squash or zucchini noodles
