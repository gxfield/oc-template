## Ingredients

- 1 medium top sirloin steak
- 1 cup mini potatoes
- 3-4 cups asparagus
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 3 Tbsp mixed herbs (rosemary, thyme, parsley)
- Salt and pepper
- 1 Tbsp avocado oil

## Instructions

1. Season steak and prick mini potatoes with a fork
2. Toss potatoes in 1 Tbsp avocado oil, rosemary, thyme, parsley, salt, and pepper
3. Bake potatoes in the oven at 425°F for 25-30 minutes
4. Cook steak on BBQ to desired doneness
5. Cook asparagus in a frying pan with 1 Tbsp butter for 5 minutes while stirring (use lid)
6. Season asparagus with salt and pepper
