## Ingredients

- Sliced ham
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- Chopped cooked bacon
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 10 eggs
- 1/2 cup sliced green onions
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and spray muffin tin with avocado oil
2. Place individual ham slices over each muffin cup and press down the middle
3. Add 1 Tbsp shredded cheese to each cup
4. Whisk eggs, green onion, bacon, blended cottage cheese, salt, and pepper together in a bowl
5. Add a couple Tbsp of egg mixture to each cup
6. Bake for 20-25 minutes
