## Ingredients

- 4 cups broccoli, chopped
- 1/2 cup plain greek yogurt or Chosen Foods mayo
- 4-5 Tbsp vinegar (to taste)
- 1/2 lemon, squeezed
- Salt and pepper
- 3 slices crispy bacon
- 1/4 cup red onion
- 1/2 cup cheddar cheese
- 1 Tbsp sunflower seeds

## Instructions

1. Cook bacon until crispy and crumble
2. Combine broccoli florets, cheese, bacon, and onion in a bowl
3. In a separate bowl, mix yogurt or mayo, vinegar, lemon juice, salt, and pepper
4. Pour dressing over broccoli, toss and serve

> Tip: For best flavor, refrigerate salad with dressing for 1 hour before serving.
