## Ingredients

- 1.5 cups cottage cheese, blended
- 4 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp stevia
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 1 cup almond flour
- 1/4 cup coconut oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients in the order given, stirring each time you add an ingredient
2. Preheat frying pan on medium heat with coconut oil
3. Pour batter into pan to form pancakes
4. Cook on each side until golden brown
