## Ingredients

- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 2 tbsp sugar-free ketchup or tomato paste
- 3 tbsp pickle juice
- 2-3 chopped up pickles
- 2 tsp white vinegar
- 2 tsp yellow mustard
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Blend cottage cheese until smooth
2. Add ketchup or tomato paste, pickle juice, chopped pickles, white vinegar, mustard, and paprika
3. Season with salt and pepper
4. Mix well and refrigerate before serving
