## Ingredients

- 2-3 boiled eggs, mashed
- 2 slices cheese (optional)
- Pickles (optional)
- Green onion (optional)
- 1-2 Tbsp Chosen Foods mayo
- Dill

## Instructions

1. Boil eggs and let cool, then mash with a fork
2. Mix in mayo, dill, and any optional ingredients
3. Assemble sandwich on flatbread or coconut wraps

> **Note:** Add flatbread or coconut wraps separately in Cronometer.
