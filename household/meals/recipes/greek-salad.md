## Ingredients

- 2 colored peppers, chopped
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- 1-2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 Tbsp feta cheese
- 2 Tbsp black olives (optional)
- 1/2 small red onion
- 1.5 cups shredded chicken or ground turkey
- Greek seasoning

## Instructions

1. Cut up and arrange veggies in a bowl
2. Add shredded chicken or ground turkey
3. Make dressing in a separate bowl
4. Drizzle dressing over veggies and gently toss
5. Add oregano and mint to taste if desired

## Nutrition

- Calories: 146 kcal

- Carbs: 8.75g
