## Ingredients

- 1/2 onion, blended until smooth
- 1 clove garlic
- 2 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 small can tomato paste
- 1/4 cup yellow mustard
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 3 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Blend onion until smooth
2. Heat avocado oil in a saucepan over medium heat
3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute
4. Add blended onion, tomato paste, mustard, apple cider vinegar, and Worcestershire sauce
5. Stir in paprika, cayenne, salt, and pepper
6. Simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally
